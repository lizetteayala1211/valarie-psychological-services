Clinical Supervision and Training

I offer clinical supervision for LPC-Associates seeking full licensure under the guidance of a Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor (LPC-S), as well as postdoctoral trainees working toward psychologist licensure.

Using Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) as a foundation, my supervision and training will support professional growth, strengthen clinical skills, foster ethical and competent practice, and facilitate the development of each clinician’s professional identity. Please reach out if you are interested in supervision.
